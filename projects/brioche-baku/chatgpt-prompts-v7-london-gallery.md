For this whole conversation you are creating photorealistic, Pinterest-worthy interior photographs of "SPRING Cake Boutique", a small modern cake boutique and tea room in Baku with a strong London neighbourhood café soul.

THE SPACE: an almost square ground-floor room of about 45 m² (approx. 7 × 6.5 m), ceiling about 3.8 m, in a 19th-century limestone building with tall rectangular street windows. Not a long narrow room.

LAYOUT (keep consistent):

- Above the counter: a minimal linear chandelier — a slim brushed-brass bar with one row of natural wooden rolling pins.
- Left wall: a long built-in sage-green leather banquette with soft vertical channels; above it a curated LONDON-STYLE GALLERY WALL (salon hang) of 14–18 framed artworks: botanical engravings (lotus, roses, cherries), small still-life oil paintings of cakes and fruit, vintage London posters, black-and-white street photographs of London and old Baku, a small portrait, two or three colourful majolica plates and one small round mirror. Mixed frames in walnut, light oak, black and thin brass, with wide cream mats. Aligned top and bottom edges, even gaps. Small round honed marble tables on slim pedestals, light-oak chairs with rounded backs.
- Centre: one round table on a sculptural turned light-oak pedestal.
- Along the windows: a built-in light-oak window bench with an oat bouclé cushion and one mustard cushion.

- Lower walls: sage-green painted wood panelling up to 1.1 m. Upper walls: warm white limewash.
- Floor: large-format warm white honed stone.
- Lighting: opal globe and paper pendants, one or two tomato-red fabric lampshades, small brass picture lights over a few artworks.

STYLE: modern warm minimalism with a London tea-room soul — calm, rounded architecture and furniture, while COLOUR and personality come from the curated artworks on the walls. Contemporary, Pinterest / Kinfolk aesthetic, collected not cluttered.

PALETTE: warm white #F4EFE6, light oak #C9A57B, walnut #6E4A33, sage green #A9B79A, cake pink #E3B8AA, tomato red #C8402F (lampshades and art), mustard #D9A53A (cushion and art), deep green #1F3B30 (small details), brushed brass. The artworks bring rich colour; architecture stays calm.
