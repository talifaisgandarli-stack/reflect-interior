## İstifadə qaydası
1. ChatGPT-də yeni söhbət açın və **0. Stil kilidi** mətnini göndərin.
2. Sonra promptları bir-bir göndərin. Pinterest üçün əsas ölçü **2:3** (şaquli) götürülüb.
3. Referans şəkilləri istəyə bağlıdır (`references/client-likes/`). Əlavə etsəniz, bunu da yazın: *"Use the attached images only as mood references; make it more modern and minimal."*
4. Nəticə ucuz və ya "plastik" görünsə, bunu əlavə edin: *"more editorial and minimal, matte, real textures, natural light and shadow, film grain."*

## 0. Stil kilidi (birinci göndərin)
```
For this whole conversation you are creating photorealistic, Pinterest-worthy interior photographs of "SPRING Cake Boutique", a small modern cake boutique and tea room in Baku.

THE SPACE: an almost square ground-floor room of about 45 m² (approx. 7 × 6.5 m), ceiling about 3.8 m, inside a 19th-century limestone building with tall rectangular street windows. Not a long narrow room.

LAYOUT (keep consistent):
- Back-right corner: a sculptural cake counter with softly rounded corners. Its base is made of two or three stacked, rounded walnut "rolls" like layers of a cake. Its top is a thick honed pink-veined marble slab. On top sits a low, frameless glass cake display.
- Back wall: a minimal built-in light-oak shelving wall with a single arched niche, hidden warm LED under the shelves, very few objects.
- Above the counter: a minimal linear chandelier — one slim brushed-brass bar with a single row of natural wooden rolling pins hanging at slightly varied heights.
- Left wall: a long built-in banquette in sage-green leather with soft vertical channels on a light-oak plinth; above it a wide frameless mirror; two simple globe wall lights; small round tables with honed marble tops on slim pedestal bases; light-oak chairs with soft rounded backs.
- Centre: one round table on a sculptural turned light-oak pedestal.
- Along the windows: a built-in light-oak window bench with a plain oat-bouclé cushion.
- Back-left corner: a small arched consultation niche in limewash.
- Walls: warm white limewash with soft texture; a low light-oak or vertical cream-tile wainscot.
- Floor: large-format warm white honed stone or seamless microcement.
- Ceiling: clean, no visible equipment; a few paper or opal globe pendants.

STYLE: modern warm minimalism with a tea-room soul — soft, rounded, stacked cake-like forms; quiet luxury through craft, not decoration; calm, light, airy, tactile. Contemporary 2025, Pinterest / Kinfolk / Cereal aesthetic.

PALETTE: warm white #F4EFE6 (40%), light oak #C9A57B (25%), walnut #6E4A33 (8%, counter only), sage green #A9B79A (12%), cake pink #E3B8AA (7%, marble and cakes), oat/beige #DCCFBD (6%), brushed brass (2%). No other colours.

MATERIALS: matte only — limewash, light oak, walnut, honed marble, sage leather, bouclé, linen, paper, opal glass, brushed brass in tiny amounts.

BRAND: the SPRING line-art lotus only very small (a cake box, the glass door, a tiny brass plaque).

PHOTOGRAPHY: Pinterest-style editorial photography — vertical frames, intentional cropping, close details, shallow depth of field, strong soft natural light with graphic shadows, real textures, warm and airy, slight film grain. Mostly no people; sometimes only a hand or a cup to suggest life.

AVOID: wide catalogue shots, symmetrical boring compositions, glossy surfaces, chrome, colourful terrazzo, patterns, stripes, florals, classic cornices inside, dark rooms, black track spotlights, clutter, bottles, neon, gold luxury, plastic CGI look, fisheye.

Reply "ready" and wait for my prompts.
```
