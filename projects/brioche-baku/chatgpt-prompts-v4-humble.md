```

MATERIALS: matte lime plaster walls, honed fine-aggregate terrazzo floor in warm white with tiny grey-green flecks (or unglazed terracotta tiles), oiled solid wood, matte handmade glazed tiles with slight colour variation, brushed stainless steel in small amounts, wool and linen upholstery in plain colours, paper or opal glass lamps. Everything matte or satin — nothing glossy.
BRAND: the SPRING line-art lotus appears only small and subtly (on the fascia, etched on the glass door, embossed on cake boxes). No lotus-pattern fabric.
PHOTOGRAPHY: quiet editorial interior photography (Kinfolk / Cereal magazine feel), natural soft daylight from the street glazing plus warm 2700K lamps, slight film grain, real imperfections and wear, generous empty space, few props, no people.
AVOID: glossy surfaces, polished chrome, vinyl chairs, bold multicolour terrazzo, busy patterns, colourful posters, many plants, globe light clusters, neon, gold, marble luxury, American diner, plastic CGI look.
```
