## 0. Stil kilidi (birinci göndərin)
```
For this whole conversation you are creating photorealistic interior visualisations for "SPRING Cake Boutique", a small cake boutique and tea room in Baku.

THE SPACE: an almost square ground-floor room of about 45 m² (approx. 7 × 6.5 m), ceiling about 3.8 m, inside a 19th-century limestone building. The whole street wall is tall glazing with rectangular openings and the entrance on the right. It is NOT a long narrow room.

LAYOUT (keep identical in every image):
- Back-right corner: the cake counter — softly rounded corners, its base made of stacked rounded walnut rings like layers of a cake (two or three soft horizontal "rolls"), topped with honed pink-veined marble; on top, a simple low glass cake display with a slim brass frame.
- Back wall behind the counter: a built-in warm amber burl-wood shelving wall with a small arched niche in the middle; shelves hold a few tea sets, cake boxes and one framed picture; a compact espresso machine.
- Above the counter: one signature chandelier made of dozens of natural wooden rolling pins hanging from a slim brass ring.
- Left wall: a long built-in banquette upholstered in sage-green leather with vertical channel tufting on a cherry-wood base; above it, a wide slightly antiqued mirror; two pleated-shade double brass wall sconces; three small round bistro tables with marble tops and brass or turned-wood bases; bentwood chairs.
- Centre: one round table for six with a sculptural turned-wood (bobbin) pedestal and solid wood chairs.
- Along the street glazing: a built-in window bench with a red-and-cream striped cushion and a few small stacked round wooden stools.
- Back-left corner: a small arched consultation niche lined in cherry wood, with a tiny round table.
- Lower walls: cherry-wood panelling or vertical cream tiles up to 1.1 m; upper walls and ceiling in warm cream matte plaster with a simple classic cornice.
- Floor: small white mosaic tiles (fan or hexagon pattern) with a thin Spring-green border.
- General light: two or three gathered cotton lampshades with thin brick-red stripes.

STYLE: "Warm Tea Room" — the soft modernism of the 1960s combined with the sincerity of a neighbourhood London–Paris tea room. Humble but beautifully crafted. Rounded, stacked, cake-like forms. Calm, warm, intimate, timeless — never flashy, never themed, never an "Instagram café".

PALETTE: cream #F2EADB (35%), warm cherry/walnut wood #8A5634 (35%), sage green #A9B79A (15%), Spring green #1F3B30 (5%, small details only), cake pink stone #D9A99A (5%), brick red #A9473A (3%, stripes only), brass (2%).

MATERIALS: matte or satin finishes only — real wood grain, honed marble, matte plaster, unglazed or satin tiles, leather, cotton and linen. Marble only on the counter top and small tables. Metal minimal (brass).

BRAND: the SPRING line-art lotus appears only small (on a cake box, etched on the glass door, a small brass plaque). No big logos.

PHOTOGRAPHY: quiet editorial interior photography (Kinfolk / Cereal), soft natural daylight from the street windows plus warm 2700K lamps (3000K inside the cake display), real imperfections, slight film grain, few props, no people.

AVOID: glossy laminate, polished chrome, multicolour terrazzo, patterned banquette, black track spotlights, 3D wall panels, industrial cage lamps, dark walls everywhere, emerald velvet and gold luxury, neon, clutter (stacked cups, bottles, collections), plastic CGI look.

Reply "ready" and wait for my prompts.
```
