## Which cake/patisserie interiors are most widely published and imitated?

### Takeaway
India Mahdavi's work is the most widely published and copied patisserie language in the world:
- Sketch Gallery, London: the pink room (2002), redone in 2022.
- The Ladurée series in Geneva, Beverly Hills, LA The Grove and Tokyo Aoyama: lattice, macaron pastels, checkered floor, scalloped and plush seating.

The Paris chef-flagship model is the second most influential:
- Cédric Grolet at Le Meurice (Ciguë, 2018) and at Opéra (Michael Malapert, 2019).
- Pierre Hermé (Christian Biecher and others).

That model is a stone counter used as a stage, with production visible and a few precious materials (brass, stone, glass bells). Peggy Porschen (London) is the most Instagram-viral "facade as backdrop" cake shop.

### Cited Findings

**1. Ladurée Quai des Bergues, Geneva (Switzerland)**
- Designer: India Mahdavi.
- Year: 2016.
- Setting: patisserie, starry tea lounge and restaurant inside a luxury Swiss hotel, over 200 m² — [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/india-mahdavi-laduree-quai-des-bergues-geneva-switzerland-12-25-2016/) [INDEXED]; [ELLE Decor](https://www.elledecor.com/it/best-of/g20692637/india-mahdavi-laduree-geneva-eng/) [INDEXED]
- Design language, in the designer's own text: "a XXI century Marie-Antoinette… 'garden of delights'".
  - Wicker armchairs, meringue-based tables, lattice work everywhere.
  - A pastel palette that refers to macarons, and a checkered floor that suggests a winter garden.
  - The same "three-dimensional new identity" was rolled out in three cities.
  - Six project photos on the page.
  - Source: [india-mahdavi.com project page](https://india-mahdavi.com/en/projects/interiors/27-laduree-quai-des-bergues) [VERIFIED]
- A Pinterest board tags the Geneva Ladurée as an "emerald green cafe interior": [Pinterest board](https://fr.pinterest.com/cosmontdesign/-cool-restaurants-/) [INDEXED, secondary]. This is relevant to SPRING's colours, but the green should be checked against the designer's photos.

**2. Ladurée Beverly Hills (USA)**
- Designer: India Mahdavi. Opening reported March 2017.
- Mahdavi's concept line: "Marie Antoinette goes to Hollywood."
- Pastel palette taken from macarons, with lattice motif, marble and a dark checkered floor.
- Sources: [Business of Home](https://businessofhome.com/articles/designer-architect-india-mahdavi-opens-two-spaces) [INDEXED]; [Domino](https://www.domino.com/content/laduree-beverly-hills/) [INDEXED]
- Mahdavi also designed Ladurée LA (The Grove) and Ladurée Aoyama in Tokyo. Aoyama has scalloped plush banquettes, twisting candy-stick tables and bespoke lighting — [Divisare – Ladurée Aoyama](https://divisare.com/projects/398802-india-mahdavi-laduree-aoyama) [INDEXED]

**3. Sketch – The Gallery, London (UK)**
- Designer: India Mahdavi.
  - The pale-pink room dates from 2002 and became an "Instagram favourite".
  - In 2022 Mahdavi redid it with artist Yinka Shonibare, for the 20th anniversary.
- 2022 scheme:
  - Sunshine-yellow and gold palette.
  - De Gournay metallic copper wallpaper.
  - Custom upholstered banquettes.
  - "Mandarine au Lait" pendant shades.
  - Ghanaian wicker wall lights by Inès Bressand.
- Mahdavi's line: "Warmth is the new colour at Sketch."
- Sources: [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/sketch-gallery-london-redesign-india-mahdavi-yinka-shonibare-03-08-2022/) [INDEXED]; [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2022/04/22/yinka-shonibare-india-mahdavi-sketch-gallery/) [INDEXED]; [The Spaces](https://thespaces.com/londons-sketch-says-goodbye-to-pink-with-new-interiors-by-india-mahdavi-and-yinka-shonibare/) [INDEXED]
- Search summaries gave conflicting numbers for the original pink. One said it "remained for eight years", but 2002–2022 is twenty years. Check the dates in the articles.

**4. Cédric Grolet Le Meurice, Paris (France)**
- Designer: Ciguë (architects' collective). Opened 20 March 2018 at 6 rue de Castiglione.
- Size: about 30 m², of which 25 m² is commercial.
- Materials: enamelled lava counter, waxed brass, Hainaut blue stone, glass bells, stucco plaster, Viabizzuno lighting.
- The long lava counter acts as a "scenic stage" for making the pastries, bringing back-of-house out to the shopfront.
- Sources: [Ciguë project page](https://cigue.net/en/?p=2181) [INDEXED]; [So Good Magazine](https://www.sogoodmagazine.com/pastry-blog/pastry-chef-articles/the-pastry-shop-le-meurice-cedric-grolet-opens-its-doors/) [INDEXED]; [france.fr](https://www.france.fr/en/article/dazzling-pastries-cedric-grolet-meurice/) [INDEXED]

**5. Cédric Grolet Opéra, Paris (France)**
- Designer: Michael Malapert. Year: 2019.
- About 300 m² (290 m² in the project info) on three floors at 35 avenue de l'Opéra, completed in 7 months.
- Brand codes: "white, grey, gold, stoned…", with a flower theme.
- Ground floor:
  - Full-height glazed shopfront, lit day and night.
  - Two stone counters facing each other: white and smooth for pastry, grey and raw for bakery.
  - Ovens on show.
  - The Haussmann shell kept: stone walls, mouldings and a big stucco rosette.
  - Forged-iron handrail with a plant motif, and metal pendants.
- First-floor tearoom:
  - Herringbone floor.
  - Broken-tile banquettes with cognac leather.
  - Brass and dark-marble bistro tables.
  - Round bar with a hammered-brass front, under a large golden round mirror.
  - Paper water-lily ceiling installation by Mathilde Nivet.
- Sources: [Archilovers – 15 photos](https://www.archilovers.com/projects/264594/cedric-grolet-opera.html) [VERIFIED]; [Neolith case study](https://www.neolith.com/neolith-projects/neolith-cedric-grolet-paris) [INDEXED]
- Tripadvisor reviewers say the interior "could use a refresh". That suggests minimalism dates quickly — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187147-d19488076-Reviews-Cedric_Grolet_Opera-Paris_Ile_de_France.html) [INDEXED]

  - Ceiling patterned with the stylised "PH" logo.
- Earlier shops: rue Bonaparte by Yann Pennor; rue Cambon and others by Olivier Lempereur.
- Source: [So Good Magazine](https://www.sogoodmagazine.com/?p=11135) [INDEXED]
- Singapore flagship by CCD (Cheng Chung Design): [Archello](https://archello.com/project/pierre-herme-paris-flagship-store) [INDEXED]; [Hospitality Snapshots](https://hospitalitysnapshots.com/projects/44220/pierre-herme-paris-flagship-store-singapore/) [INDEXED]
- Champs-Élysées shop shared with L'Occitane (2018), by Laura Gonzalez: [Domus](https://www.domusweb.it/en/news/2018/01/25/pierre-herm--loccitane-a-boutique-inspired-by-czanne-and-nature.preview.html) [INDEXED]

**7. Ladurée Champs-Élysées, Paris (France)**
- Designer: Jacques Garcia. Year: 1997. Style: Second Empire.
- Ladurée's wider decorative language: tassels, velvet, mouldings, old paintings.
- Sources: [Ladurée history](https://www.laduree.fr/en/laduree-160-years-of-delicious-history.html) [INDEXED]; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladur%C3%A9e) [INDEXED]

**8. Dalloyau Faubourg Saint-Honoré, Paris (France)**
- Designer: Tihany Design. Reopened in 2026 under Potel & Chabot. The house was founded in 1682.
- Materials: Versailles parquet, mouldings, alabaster, brass, solid oak, Calacatta Oro marble, giving a quiet "mansion" feel.

- Facade: the dark arcades and pale-blue livery were restored to their original look.

**9. Peggy Porschen, Belgravia, London (UK)**

- The brand is "built on Peggy's love of a pink and pastel colour palette, modern romance", and offers a "fairy-tale setting".

**10. Demel, Kohlmarkt, Vienna (Austria)**

**11. La Pâtisserie des Rêves, Paris (France) and Kyoto (Japan)**

### Inferences
- **The most imitated pattern** is Mahdavi's Ladurée "garden" vocabulary: lattice, macaron pastels, checkerboard floor, scalloped velvet seating and wicker. Pastel-pink cake cafés in the Gulf and CIS clearly come from Ladurée, Sketch and Peggy Porschen.
- **The second most imitated pattern** is the Grolet "jewellery store for pastry": a single stone counter, few pastries, pieces under glass bells, and brass details.
- **For SPRING**, mixing the two approaches looks strongest:
  - Grolet Opéra / Dalloyau: keep the historic shell, insert a monolithic stone counter, use brass.
  - Mahdavi: an emotional colour story, with emerald replacing pastel.

### Gaps
- **Dominique Ansel (NYC) and Bachour (Miami):** no reliable interior-design sources found. Both are famous for products, not interiors.
- **Lenôtre and Maison Pradier:** not researched in depth; no designer credits found.
- **Café Sacher Vienna:** no interior sources found in this pass. The red damask look is well known, but no source was retrieved.
- **Image check:** most links were not scraped (rate limits and a blocked proxy), so check image galleries by hand before putting them in client decks.

## Which use deep/emerald green with gold/brass, florals, or are set within historic façades?

### Takeaway
No well-published flagship patisserie that is mainly emerald and gold turned up in sources (searches came back generic). The closest documented precedents fall into two groups:
- **Historic shell with brass and stone inserted:** Grolet Opéra (Haussmann shell, hammered brass, gold mirror), Dalloyau (restored facade, brass, Calacatta Oro), Le Meurice (waxed brass, blue stone), Demel (neo-baroque gold).
- **Floral or garden themes:** Mahdavi's Ladurée "garden of delights", Grolet's paper water-lily ceiling, and Peggy Porschen's flower-framed facade.

Grolet's water lilies are an especially close match for SPRING's lotus logo.

### Cited Findings
- **Grolet Opéra:**
  - Haussmann stone walls, mouldings and stucco rosette kept.
  - Hammered-brass bar front, large golden round mirror, brass and dark-marble tables.
  - Ceiling of handmade paper water lilies (grey to light pink) by Mathilde Nivet.
  - Source: [Archilovers](https://www.archilovers.com/projects/264594/cedric-grolet-opera.html) [VERIFIED]
- **Dalloyau:** historic facade (dark arcades, pale-blue livery) restored, with brass, alabaster, Calacatta Oro and Versailles parquet inside — [Sortiraparis](https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/where-to-eat-in-paris/brunch-cafe-tea-time/articles/351359-the-dalloyau-pastry-shop-and-tea-room-on-saint-honore-is-being-reborn-and-undergoing-a-complete-transformation) [VERIFIED page]
- **Le Meurice:** waxed brass, Hainaut blue stone, glass bells inside a historic Paris hotel building — [Ciguë](https://cigue.net/en/?p=2181) [INDEXED]
- **Ladurée Geneva:** a garden-and-lattice concept. One secondary board tags it as an emerald-green café — [India Mahdavi](https://india-mahdavi.com/en/projects/interiors/27-laduree-quai-des-bergues) [VERIFIED]; [Pinterest](https://fr.pinterest.com/cosmontdesign/-cool-restaurants-/) [INDEXED]
- **Katana Bakery, Riyadh:** Calacatta marble, bleached oak, brass, Venetian plaster ceiling, and a bespoke brass ceiling sculpture above the central cake showcase — [Blenheim Design](https://www.blenheimdesign.com/projects/katana-bakery-riyadh/) [VERIFIED, 10+ project images]
- **Haute Dolci, Kuwait (Hawally):** "champagne, blue, and gold" palette, seats 124 — [Haute Dolci Kuwait](https://hautedolci.co.uk/stores/kuwait/) [INDEXED]
- **Demel, Vienna:** neo-baroque gold, marble and chandeliers in its original 19th-century building — [Demel history](https://demel.com/en/pages/demels-history) [INDEXED]
- **Angelina, Paris** (rue de Rivoli tea house): a historic tearoom reference — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelina_(tea_house)) [INDEXED]. The retrieved snippet did not describe its décor.
- **Café Pushkin confectionery, Moscow:** in a 19th-century building on Tverskoy Boulevard. The ground-floor confectionery leads to upper rooms with period detail — [En Primeur Club](https://www.enprimeurclub.com/restaurants/confectionary-pushkin-moscow-restaurant) [INDEXED]

### Inferences
- SPRING's limestone facade and emerald-and-gold identity match the "historic shell, modern precious insert" strategy (Grolet Opéra, Dalloyau). Possible translation:
  - Keep the limestone and mouldings.
  - Add an emerald element such as a lacquered or Verde Guatemala marble counter.
  - Use brushed or hammered brass trim, plus a golden mirror or lotus-shaped ceiling element (after Grolet's water lilies).
- Emerald works with white and bright gold. One general design source warns against using it on every surface: [Wallsauce](https://www.wallsauce.com/us/blog/emerald-green-home-decor-ideas) [INDEXED]

### Gaps
- No published patisserie flagship whose main palette is emerald and gold was found. That leaves room for SPRING to be distinctive, but there is no direct precedent to cite.
- **Ladurée's colours:** its own pistachio-mint/gold livery was not confirmed by a retrieved source.

## Strong regional examples (Middle East, Turkey, CIS, Caucasus) relevant to Baku

### Takeaway
Few regional cake-boutique interiors are covered by design media. The best-documented ones:
- Katana Bakery, Riyadh (Blenheim Design, 2020).
- Haute Dolci, Kuwait (champagne, blue and gold).
- Pierre Hermé at Four Seasons Riyadh (marble mosaic).
- La Nena, Dubai (designboom).
- ORIGAMI bakery, Istanbul.
- Historic Istanbul and Moscow patisseries: Lebon by Vallaury, Patisserie de Pera, Baylan, Café Pushkin.

Nothing design-documented turned up for Almaty, Tbilisi or Doha.

### Inferences
- **Gulf luxury cake boutiques** tend to use Calacatta marble, brass and champagne-gold, with pastel or blue accents (Katana, Haute Dolci, Pierre Hermé Riyadh). An emerald-and-gold SPRING would sit in the same luxury register while standing out.
- **Istanbul and Moscow** show that heritage patisseries in historic buildings (Lebon, Pera, Café Pushkin, Demel as a European parallel) are valued as institutions. That suits SPRING's historic limestone facade in Baku.

### Gaps
- **Almaty, Tbilisi, Doha:** no design-documented luxury cake boutique interiors found.
- **Baku itself:** no competitor interiors researched; out of scope for this pass.
- **Instagram reach:** no figures (follower counts, hashtag volumes) were found for any venue, so "viral" is judged qualitatively from how much press and how many user photos each one has.
