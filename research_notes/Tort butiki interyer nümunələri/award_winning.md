# Award-winning / award-listed patisserie, cake boutique and dessert shop interiors (2015–2026), with references for SPRING Cake Boutique, Baku

Verification method: every link marked **[verified]** was scraped on 2026-10-19 with Firecrawl and returned HTTP 200 with project photos on the page (photo counts are in brackets). ## Q1. Which patisserie/cake boutique interiors have won or been listed for major design awards?

### Takeaway
Pure patisserie interiors rarely win the "big three" (Dezeen, FRAME, Prix Versailles) outright. The strongest confirmed results are: **Nanan** (on the Restaurant & Bar Design Awards 2017 winners page), **Charlotte by PA Patisserie** (Interior Design Best of Year 2019 plus a Golden A' Design Award), **Cukiernia Lviv** (Architecture MasterPrize 2024 winner), **Kova** (Dezeen Awards 2020 longlist) and **Umaga Bakehouse** (RBDA 2025 shortlist). Most of the remaining awarded examples come from A' Design Award, INT, MUSE, IHPA and Best Awards (NZ).

### Cited Findings — project list (16 projects: 13 award-linked, 3 strong design references with no award found)

**1. NANAN Patisserie, Wrocław, Poland. BUCK.studio, 2016**
- Award: listed with an image on the **Restaurant & Bar Design Awards "Winners 2017"** archive page. The scrape did not capture the exact category. — [RBDA Winners 2017](https://restaurantandbardesignawards.com/awards-archive/2017) [verified]. A search snippet also reports it as a 2017 HD (Hospitality Design) award winner/finalist — [HD 2017 slideshow](https://hospitalitydesign.com/projects/2017-hd-project-award-finalists/101377/slideshow/24) [search-only; scrape was rate-limited].
- Design: deliberately minimalist; pink velvet walls, brass, veneer, bespoke lamps. A **central oval island counter whose display "operates almost like a glass cabinet in a jeweller's shop"**, echoing the shape of an éclair. — [Archello: NANAN Patisserie](https://archello.com/project/nanan-patisserie) [verified, 17 photos]

**2. Charlotte by PA Patisserie, Wuxi, China. OYTT Design (Ouyang Tiao), 2019**
- Awards: **Interior Design magazine 2019 Best of Year Winner, Counter Service** — [Interior Design](https://interiordesign.net/projects/charlotte-by-pa-patisserie-by-oytt-design-2019-best-of-year-winner-for-counter-service/) [verified, 3 photos]. **2019 Golden A' Design Award**, Interior Space — [A' Design Award page](https://competition.adesignaward.com/ada-winner-design.php?ID=72014) [search-only].
- Design: crisp white, **pine-green** and "Christmas red"; a sculpted ceiling plane that swoops down to become the walls; service bar topped by a **smooth terrazzo counter**; expansive glazing; hanging lanterns and a glowing logo. — [Interior Design](https://interiordesign.net/projects/charlotte-by-pa-patisserie-by-oytt-design-2019-best-of-year-winner-for-counter-service/)

**3. Cukiernia, Lviv, Ukraine. YOD Group, 2024**
- Award: **Architecture MasterPrize 2024, winner, Interior Design / Interior Architecture** — [architectureprize.com](https://architectureprize.com/winners/winner.php?id=7558) [verified, 11 photos]. A press kit says it was "Best of Best" — [v2com press kit](https://www.v2com-newswire.com/en/newsroom/press-kits/6673-02/cukiernia-the-pastry-shop-with-walls-covered-in-eggshell-plaster) [search-only]. Frame also has a project page — [frameweb.com/project/cukiernia](https://frameweb.com/project/cukiernia) [search-only; it reportedly shows a "Bronze" label, unconfirmed].
- Design: a reimagining of an iconic pastry shop in Lviv's historic centre. Italian marble with "caramel-like veins" on the floors and **countertops**, and a **custom eggshell plaster made from 1,500 kg of recycled shells** on the walls. Three halls with clean lines and **subtle arches**, restored vintage furniture, and minimalist lighting that puts the focus on the pastries. — [architectureprize.com](https://architectureprize.com/winners/winner.php?id=7558)

**4. Kova South Kensington, London, UK. Atelier Baulier, 2019**
- Award: **Dezeen Awards 2020 longlist, small interior**. — [Dezeen Awards page](https://www.dezeen.com/awards/2020/longlists/kova-south-kensington/) [verified, 1 large photo]
- Design: **a narrow unit**: a generous seating area faces a counter that "displays the shop's offerings with clarity". Inspired by Japanese gardens; tonal greys and pink set against the bright green matcha signature cake. — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/awards/2020/longlists/kova-south-kensington/). Other reported details: flint terrazzo counter, clay-render walls, bolster banquette, and perforated recycled-PET acoustic panels — [Hospitality Snapshots](https://hospitalitysnapshots.com/projects/5214/kova-south-kensington/) [search-only]

**5. Umaga Bakehouse, Chicago, USA. AGN Design (Aida Napoles), 2025**
- Award: **Restaurant & Bar Design Awards 2025 shortlist, Americas – Café**. — [Time Out Chicago](https://www.timeout.com/chicago/news/this-beautiful-chicago-bakery-was-just-shortlisted-at-the-2025-restaurant-bar-design-awards-082225) [verified, 2 photos]
- Design: warm earth tones, bronze tile accents, floor-to-ceiling windows, and a **bespoke S-shaped wooden table that doubles as seating and display**. (This is a bakery and café rather than a cake boutique.)

**6. Capitol Bistro. Bar. Patisserie, Singapore. DP Design (Mike Lim)**
- Award: listed under Commercial on the **INT Interior Design Awards 2026 winners** list — [INT winners](https://intdesignaward.com/winners/) [search snippet]; project page — [INT project page](https://intdesignaward.com/winners/winner.php?id=22-93466-26&count=0&mode=zoom) [verified, 11 photos]

A "tropical oasis" concept with as much natural light as possible, bringing the outside in. — [INT page](https://intdesignaward.com/winners/winner.php?id=22-93466-26&count=0&mode=zoom)

**7. Atelier de Tartelettes, Moscow, Russia. MEGRE Interiors, 2020**
- Award: International Hotel & Property Awards 2020, Restaurant – Europe. The page slug reads "restaurant-europe-award-2020", but a search summary said "shortlisted". **Treat this as shortlisted unless confirmed.** — [The Design Society](https://thedesignsoc.com/megre-interiors-restaurant-europe-award-2020/) [verified, 4 photos]
- Design: gold and white; plastered gypsum, stone, brass and mirrors; custom crisp white counters; works with limited natural light.

**8. Folks Patisserie Cafe, Uzhhorod, Ukraine. Tetyana Monics, 2016–2018**
- Award: **A' Design Award 2018–2019, Interior Space** (the level is not shown on the scraped page). — [A' Design Award](https://competition.adesignaward.com/design.php?ID=79190) [verified, 5 photos]

**9. Chuchulab Short Cakes Dessert Shop, Taiwan (location not stated). Hhhhsstudio Design Co., 2026**

### Inferences
- The A' Design Award gives the most "award-winning" cake and dessert shops by count, but it is a paid-entry programme with a high acceptance rate. For a client presentation, RBDA, Interior Design Best of Year, Architecture MasterPrize, Dezeen and INT carry more weight.
- Charlotte, Nanan, Cukiernia and Kova are the most defensible "award-winning patisserie" precedents to show.

### Gaps
- An RBDA entry page "Cake Bloom" (entry 11261, 2021 Café shortlist, 9 photos) loads, but the sources contradict each other: a search snippet said Dubai / KOCKA design, while the page scrape said Charlottesville / Studio Plow. It was left out of the list until checked by hand: https://restaurantandbardesignawards.com/entry/11261
- No FRAME Awards or Prix Versailles winner that is specifically a patisserie or cake boutique was found. Both programmes award shops and restaurants broadly. Neither has a dessert category, and none of their winners turned up as a patisserie.
- No Golden Pin, iF or Red Dot winner that is specifically a patisserie was confirmed.
- Not verified because of scrape rate limits: Adriano Zumbo Pâtisserie Melbourne (reported Eat Drink Design Awards shortlist, Best Retail Design — [bakingbusiness.com.au](https://bakingbusiness.com.au/baking-industry-has-a-taste-for-fine-design/) [search-only]); Judy's Patisserie (MUSE winner — [MUSE](https://design.museaward.com/winners-info.php?id=1673) [search-only]); Fuwa Fuwa Dessert Cafe (LIV Hospitality Honorable Mention — [LIV](https://livawards.com/winner/hm/4/131-53654-24/0/) [search-only]); Le Temps Patisserie (A' winner — [A'](https://competition.adesignaward.com/design.php?ID=73231) [search-only]).

## Q2. What design moves (vitrines, counters, lighting, materials) are characteristic?

### Takeaway
The recurring formula is a single **sculptural, jewel-box counter or island** as the hero, set in a restrained shell (one dominant colour plus one metal), with warm, controlled lighting that makes the vitrine the brightest object in the room.

### Cited Findings
- **Display as jewellery cabinet**: Nanan's oval island display "operates almost like a glass cabinet in a jeweller's shop". — [Archello](https://archello.com/project/nanan-patisserie)
- **Central island counter for circulation**: Nanan, Chuchulab ("central island bar that optimizes spatial circulation"). — [Archello](https://archello.com/project/nanan-patisserie); [A'](https://competition.adesignaward.com/ada-winner-design.php?ID=183322)
- **Stone counter tops**: terrazzo (Charlotte; Kova's flint terrazzo) and veined marble (Cukiernia, Maison Féerie with brass edging). — [Interior Design](https://interiordesign.net/projects/charlotte-by-pa-patisserie-by-oytt-design-2019-best-of-year-winner-for-counter-service/); [architectureprize](https://architectureprize.com/winners/winner.php?id=7558); [Archello](https://archello.com/project/maison-feerie)
- **Brass as the accent metal**: Nanan, Folks (brass floor inlay and tubes), Atelier de Tartelettes, Maison Féerie. — sources as above
- **Textured, soft wall finishes**: velvet (Nanan), eggshell plaster (Cukiernia), clay render (Kova), rammed earth (Sweet Interstellar).
- **Arches and curves**: Cukiernia ("subtle arches"), Chuchulab (tidal curves), Charlotte (ceiling sweeping into the walls).
- **Lighting**: bespoke pendants (Nanan), gold pendants (Folks), lanterns and a glowing logo (Charlotte), "minimalist lighting… emphasizing pastries" (Cukiernia).
- **Narrow plans**: Kova "makes the most of a narrow space": counter along one side, seating facing it. — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/awards/2020/longlists/kova-south-kensington/)

### Inferences
- For a narrow, deep unit like SPRING's, the Kova model (a linear counter-vitrine along one long wall with seating opposite) and the Nanan model (a free-standing jewel island deeper in the plan, pulling people past the glazing) are the two proven layouts.

### Gaps
- Specific vitrine manufacturers and lighting colour temperatures were not stated on the verified pages.

## Q3. Which use deep green, gold/brass, botanical motifs, or sit in historic buildings? (relevance to SPRING: emerald green and gold, lotus logo, limestone facade)

### Takeaway
The closest matches for SPRING are **Gard'Ann** (deep green, gold, botanical mural, 1928 heritage interior; no award), **Capitol Bistro. Bar. Patisserie** (conserved heritage building, tropical green; INT 2026 winner), **Cukiernia** (historic Lviv, arches, stone, Architecture MasterPrize), **Charlotte** (pine green and terrazzo; Best of Year and Golden A') and **Maison Féerie** (deep green, marble and brass; no award).

### Cited Findings
- **Deep green + gold + botanical + heritage**: Gard'Ann: "complementarity between the deep green, the golden tones and the pastels"; a mural of "leaves, plants and birds"; 1928 heritage-protected furnishings. — [kissmiklos.com](https://kissmiklos.com/gard-ann-patisserie)
- **Deep green + marble + brass**: Maison Féerie. — [Archello](https://archello.com/project/maison-feerie)
- **Green + award**: Charlotte by PA Patisserie (pine-green). — [Interior Design](https://interiordesign.net/projects/charlotte-by-pa-patisserie-by-oytt-design-2019-best-of-year-winner-for-counter-service/)
- **Botanical/greenery + award**: Chuchulab ("lush greenery", Silver A' 2026); Capitol ("tropical oasis", INT 2026). — [A'](https://competition.adesignaward.com/ada-winner-design.php?ID=183322); [INT](https://intdesignaward.com/winners/winner.php?id=22-93466-26&count=0&mode=zoom)
- **Historic or heritage building + award**: Capitol (conserved Capitol Building, Singapore); Cukiernia (iconic Lviv pastry shop); Folks (renovated older building); Guss Design Patisserie (stone walls, "historically rich" atmosphere). — sources as above
- **Gold/brass + award**: Nanan (brass and pink velvet), Folks (brass and gold pendants), Atelier de Tartelettes (gold and white, brass). — sources as above

### Inferences
- For SPRING's limestone facade, the Cukiernia approach (stone and plaster textures, arches, letting the historic shell show) combined with Gard'Ann's palette logic (deep green field plus gold accents plus a botanical narrative carried through to packaging) is the most transferable pairing. A lotus motif could play the role Gard'Ann's bird-and-leaf mural plays.
- Brass-inlaid floor patterns (Folks) suggest one way to inlay the lotus logo into the floor or the counter front.

### Gaps
- No verified award-winning project combines **emerald green, gold and a historic stone facade** in a single cake boutique. Gard'Ann is the nearest match but has no confirmed award.
- Ladurée Geneva (India Mahdavi, in the historic Hotel des Bergues, reported green and mauve palette) and Marchesi 1824 Milan (historic Galleria, green) are relevant precedents, but no award or verified photo link was established for them in this session.
