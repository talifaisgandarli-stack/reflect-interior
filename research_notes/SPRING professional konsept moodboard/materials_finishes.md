## 1. Green stones for counters and tabletops (Verde Guatemala, Verde Alpi, Verde Antico, Rainforest Green, Verde Lapponia, Connemara) and sintered/porcelain alternatives

### Takeaway
The commercially common "green marbles" (Verde Guatemala/Indian Green, Rainforest Green, Verde Alpi) are mostly **serpentinites**, not calcite marbles. They are harder and less acid-etch-prone than true marble, but they still need sealing. Specify a **honed** finish for counters. For the highest-abuse surfaces (service counter, cake-cutting tops), a **Laminam "I Naturali" Verde Alpi** sintered slab or the **Fiandre (Iris group) Maximum Marmi "Alpi Chiaro Venato"** porcelain slab gives the Verde Alpi look with no etching risk.

### Cited Findings
**Verde Guatemala / Indian Green (Rajasthan)**
- The stone sold as Verde Guatemala is Indian. The name comes from an older Guatemalan green stone that became unavailable, and a similar Indian serpentinite replaced it in the trade — [Magna Atelier](https://www.magna-atelier.de/en/blogs/news/im-fokus-der-verde-guatemala)
- It is technically serpentinite (a metamorphic ultramafic rock), not limestone-based marble — [Magna Atelier](https://www.magna-atelier.de/en/blogs/news/im-fokus-der-verde-guatemala); [SlabWise](https://slabwise.com/guides/green-marble-countertops)
- Deposits are in the Aravalli range. Udaipur firms work mostly southern-Rajasthan stone — [Litos Online](https://litosonline.com/en/article/green-marble-india)
- Belgian distributor BMB sells "Verde Radjastan" in honed, "verzoet" (softened) and sand-brushed finishes. These are useful references for a matte F&B finish — [BMB honed](https://www.bmb.be/en/artikelen/142551/verde-radjastan-honed); [BMB sand brush](https://www.bmb.be/en/artikelen/142600/verde-radjastan-sand-brush). Brachot (Belgium) lists it as "Vert de Rajasthan" — [Brachot](https://www.brachot.com/en/materials/raja/vert-de-rajasthan/)
- StoneContact recommends sealing Verde Guatemala countertops after cleaning to prevent future staining — [StoneContact](https://www.stonecontact.com/verde-guatemala-marble/s111)

**Rainforest Green (Bidasar Green)**
- Rainforest Green (also called Bidasar Green) comes from Bidasar, about 250 km from Jaipur. It has a medium-green base with gold/brown veins in tree-like patterns — [Flodeal](https://flodeal.com/blog/indian-green-marble-verde-guatemala); [SlabWise](https://slabwise.com/guides/green-marble-countertops)

**Etching / sealing / finish (general green stone)**
- Serpentinite is harder than limestone-based marble and less prone to acid etching. An acid test can confirm a slab's mineralogy — [SlabWise](https://slabwise.com/guides/green-marble-countertops)
- On a honed surface etches "nearly disappear" because there is no gloss to contrast with. Specify honed and seal twice a year. Citrus, vinegar and wine will etch true marble over time — [SlabWise](https://slabwise.com/guides/green-marble-countertops)
- Honed is the usual professional recommendation for marble, to reduce visible acid etching. Leathered is the usual recommendation for dark granite — [Architectural Surfaces](https://arcsurfaces.com/blogs/countertop-finishes-complete-guide/)
- A protective film product (AM3 "Marble without fear") claims to stop etching and staining on honed or polished marble — [AM3 Stone](https://am3stone.com/marble-without-fear-of-etching-staining/) (manufacturer claim)

**Verde Alpi (Valle d'Aosta, Italy)**
- Verde Alpi is a deep-green brecciated serpentine with black and white areas, quarried in Valle d'Aosta — [Pinterest/Laminam pin](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/laminam-verde-alpi--430938258102575006/) (secondary). The name is often used generically for northern-Italian serpentinites — [Elite Stone](https://www.elitestone.it/materials/verde-alpi-marble/); [Dedalo Stone](https://www.dedalostone.com/en/materials/verde-alpi-green-marble/)
- Technical values conflict between suppliers. Compressive strength is about 1,876–1,945 kg/cm², water absorption about 0.7% (another source gives 0.0095), and bulk density about 2,680 kg/m³ — [Materials Assemble](https://materialsassemble.com/materials-library/marble-stones/marble/verde-alpi-marble); [Elite Stone](https://www.elitestone.it/materials/verde-alpi-marble/)
- Materials Assemble lists Verde Alpi under "sustainable materials for hospitality" — [Materials Assemble](https://materialsassemble.com/materials-library/uses/sustainable-materials-for-hospitality/verde-alpi-marble/)

**Sintered / porcelain alternatives**
- **Laminam I Naturali Verde Alpi**: a sintered surface reinterpreting Verde Alpi, in 1200×3000 mm format. Product page: [Laminam](https://www.laminam.com/en/products/i-naturali-verde-alpi/). A distributor lists a 12 mm thickness, resistant to scratches, frost and UV — [AUSKstone](https://www.auskstone.com.au/shop/20mm-sintered-stone/laminam/laminam-group-3/verde-alpi-laminam-sintered-stone/); a US distributor sells samples — [Ollin Stone](https://ollinstone.com/product/verde-alpi-laminam-slabs/)
- **Fiandre (Iris Ceramica group) Maximum Marmi**: the colours include **Alpi Chiaro Venato** (intense green ground with white veins, for cladding or flooring), **Verde Fantastico** and **Verde Persia**. Slabs are 6 mm thick — [Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/products/surfaces/maximum-marmi-iris-ceramica)
- **Atlas Concorde Atlas Plan**: its Natura-Vein technology produces through-body veining, and green is among its colour families. No specific green product name was found — [Interni](https://www.internimagazine.com/annual-en/annual-cucina-2023-en/atlas-plan-il-gres-porcellanato-in-cucina/); [Atlas Plan FAQ](https://www.atlasplan.com/en/faq/)
- **Neolith**: I found no current Verde-named model. An older design, **Amazonico**, is described as a greenish exotic-marble look in "Decor Polished" — [Surfaces International / search result](https://surfacesinternational.com/materials/wall-and-floor-coverings/neolith-nature-and-new-sintered-stone-declinations) **[UNVERIFIED whether still in production]**. Neolith's 2025 launch (Iconic Design: Victoria, El Estatuario) has no green model — [KBB Focus](https://kbbfocus.com/news/5083-latest-neolith-collection-represents-a-new-generation-of-sintered-stone-surfaces)
- **Dekton (Cosentino)**: no green-marble model found in this search — [KBB Focus](https://kbbfocus.com/news/4801-neolith-impresses-with-innovative-new-products-at-kbis-2025) (context only)
- **Smartstone Calacatta Verde** (sintered): moss-to-emerald veins on a grey-white ground. Its fit with the brand palette is questionable — [search result via KBB/Smartstone] **[UNVERIFIED URL]**
- An Italian forum post claims Dekton "leaves a mark with heat." This is anecdotal and unreliable — [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/istrutturare/comments/1s24vbk/top_cucina_in_gres_laminam_vs_dekton_vs_neolith/?tl=en)

### Inferences
- **Recommended schedule:**
  - Primary counter or vitrine plinth top: Verde Guatemala or Verde Alpi, **honed**, sealed with an impregnating sealer, 20–30 mm with a mitred edge.
  - Customer tabletops: Laminam Verde Alpi or Fiandre Alpi Chiaro Venato (zero etch, food-safe, heat-tolerant).
  - Rainforest Green: avoid as the hero stone. Its brown/gold veining reads warmer and more "rustic" than #0F2A22, though it could suit small accents.
- Verde Guatemala is the colour closest to #0F2A22: a very dark green with white calcite veins.
- Leathered finishes on serpentinite hide etching and fingerprints well, but leave open texture that can trap buttercream or chocolate. Use honed on food-contact surfaces and leathered only on vertical or decorative faces.
- Price tier (estimate): Indian Green is low–mid (Indian export volume is high, and it is widely stocked in Turkey and the UAE). Verde Alpi is mid–high. Laminam and Fiandre slabs are mid–high (Italian import).
- Baku climate: the stone needs no special treatment. But serpentinite slabs can have fissures and mesh-backing, so ask for resin-backed slabs. Avoid placing any stone or sintered top in direct sun against glazing in summer, to reduce differential thermal stress on joints.

### Gaps
- **Verde Antico, Verde Lapponia, Irish Connemara**: no sourced technical or supplier data was found. Connemara is a serpentine-bearing marble from Ireland, with limited slab availability **[UNVERIFIED]**. Verde Lapponia is a Finnish green quartzite/granite type **[UNVERIFIED]**, which would be acid-resistant if so.
- No sourced Baku, Istanbul or Dubai price lists for any green stone.
- I did not open the Laminam product page to confirm thickness options or the current image URL.

## 2. Accent stones: Rosa Portogallo, Calacatta Viola, Turkish Denizli travertine, Azerbaijani limestone

### Takeaway
Rosa Portogallo (Estremoz, Portugal) and Calacatta Viola (Italy) are both true calcite marbles. They will etch, so use them only on low-contact accents (arch reveals, wall inlays, display niches). Denizli travertine is the most practical regional warm stone, with established Turkish exporters. No "Gilavar" limestone brand exists in the sources: Gilavar is the name of a Baku wind. Local Absheron limestone (e.g., from quarries near Türkan and the Garadagh deposit) is documented as Baku's historic building stone.

### Cited Findings
**Rosa Portogallo**
- A pink marble from the Estremoz region of Portugal. Artistic Tile sells it as 2 cm honed or polished slabs and 12×24" honed or polished field tiles. US retail prices are about $45/sf for honed tile and $72/sf for honed slab — [Artistic Tile slab](https://artistictile.com/products/rosa-portogallo-slrporth2); [Artistic Tile collection](https://www.artistictile.com/collections/rosa-portogallo)
- Material Bank lists China as the country of origin for the same tile, which conflicts with the Estremoz origin. Verify provenance — [Material Bank](https://www.materialbank.com/artistic-tile-rosa-portogallo-834918)
- Frictional wear is about 8 mm — [Materials Assemble](https://materialsassemble.com/materials-library/uses/sustainable-materials-for-hospitality/rosa-portogallo-marble/)

**Calacatta Viola**
- A white fine-grained marble with broad purple-red veining. It has natural imperfections and surface fissures — [Brachot](https://www.brachot.com/en/materials/calvio/calacatta-viola/)

**Denizli travertine (Turkey)**
- Denizli is Turkey's leading travertine region. A 2006 paper counted about 40 quarries and 50 factories, exporting to about 60 countries — [IAEG 2006 paper](https://media.geolsoc.org.uk/iaeg2006/PAPERS/IAEG_384.PDF)
- Grades include Denizli Classic and Light. Finishes are polished, honed and filled — [Stone-Ideas](https://www.stone-ideas.com/110515/denizli-travertine-from-turkey/)
- Producers:
  - AG Demirok: cross and vein cut; polished, filled, honed and brushed finishes — [Turkish Exporter](https://www.turkishexporter.com.tr/en/companies/ag-demirok-33261/)
  - Kömürcüoğlu: own quarries — [World Architecture](https://worldarchitecture.org/architecture-materials/companies/pvvvp/komurcuoglu_marble_co_-materials-company.html)
  - İlci — [ilci.com.tr](https://ilci.com.tr/maden?lang=en)
  - Alimoğlu exports to GCC markets, among others (via search summary)

**Azerbaijani limestone**
- "Gilavar" is the name of a warm southern wind in Baku and Shamakhi, not a stone — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilavar)
- Local limestones were used historically in Baku's Old City (İçərişəhər). An MDPI study tests their behaviour under heat — [MDPI Fire 2023](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-6255/6/7/263)
- A study assesses the Garadagh limestone deposit as an industrial stone resource — [DOI](https://www.doi.org/10.36719/2663-4619/78/226-230)
- RFE/RL reported on quarries cutting stone for construction in Türkan, on Baku's outskirts — [RFE/RL](https://rferl.org/a/24277333.html)
- Construction limestone output: 94,700 t in Jan–Feb 2026, up 61.3% year on year. Full-year 2025 output was 309,000 t — [Report.az](https://report.az/en/industry/azerbaijan-doubles-construction-limestone-production-in-february)

### Inferences
- Use local Absheron sawn limestone (shell limestone, "mişar daşı") to echo the historic beige facade. Good places are the entry threshold reveals, arch surrounds and a plinth band. It is porous and soft, so seal it and keep it away from horizontal food-contact surfaces. Price tier: low.
- Denizli travertine (filled, honed) suits a warm floor border or the vitrine base. It reaches Baku by road and rail through Georgia, or by sea and road via the Black Sea. Price tier: low–mid. Specify filled pores with cementitious or epoxy filler for a food environment.
- Rosa Portogallo is a soft blush accent that harmonises with gold and works well for lotus-petal inlays in the floor (water-jet cut). Calacatta Viola is bolder and risks clashing with the emerald. Use it only for a single statement element, such as a cake-display pedestal top with a glass cover.
- Both pink and violet marbles etch, so put them on vertical surfaces or protect them with an anti-etch film.

### Gaps
- No named Baku limestone quarry company or product with test data was found. A "Ganja" stone product name was not verified.
- No sourced price for Calacatta Viola or Denizli travertine delivered to Baku.

## 3. Terrazzo (Mipa, Diespeker, Agglotech, Marmoreal) — green and gold chip specs; poured vs tile

### Takeaway
**Agglotech** (Italy) has documented green terrazzo slabs (Smeraldo SB157, Verde, Verde Scuro) and a cement-based, resin-free system. **Dzek Marmoreal** by Max Lamb comes in only two grounds (white and black), each with Verde Alpi inclusions. The black version fits a dark green-gold scheme. No data on Mipa or Diespeker was found.

### Cited Findings
- Agglotech describes itself as Europe's largest terrazzo producer, with more than 4,000 colours and honed, polished, brushed and bush-hammered finishes — [Material Bank EU](https://www.materialbank.eu/partner/agglotech); [Fuorisalone](https://www.fuorisalone.it/en/brand/1881/AGGLOTECH-SPA-SB)
- Agglotech green products:
  - **Terrazzo Smeraldo (SB157)**: turtle-green ground with marble, granite and recycled-pebble chips. Slabs are 2 or 3 cm thick and 2.53 × 1.42 m — [Worktop Library](https://worktoplibrary.co.uk/?p=24168)
  - **Terrazzo Verde**: green ground with black, grey and beige chips of 2–12 mm — [Worktop Library](https://worktoplibrary.co.uk/?p=24151)
  - **Verde Scuro**: dark green with lighter green speckles. Marketed for worktops and high-traffic floors — [Worktop Library category](https://worktoplibrary.co.uk/product-category/terrazzo/agglotech/)
- Agglotech's catalogue describes marble aggregate mixed with water and 52.5 Portland cement **without resins**. A retailer claims a resin binder instead, which conflicts. Trust the catalogue — [Agglotech catalogue PDF](https://worktoplibrary.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/AGGLOTECH-Architectural-Terrazzo-Catalogue-Colours.pdf)
- **Dzek Marmoreal** by Max Lamb:
  - It has a white version (Bianco Verona ground) and a black version. Both have Verde Alpi, Giallo Mori and Rosso Verona inserts.
  - It is about 95% marble and 5% polyester resin.
  - Dzek says it is for interior architectural surfaces. MaterialDistrict says it also works outdoors.
  - Sources: [Dzek](https://dzekdzekdzek.com/marmoreal); [MaterialDistrict](https://materialdistrict.com/material/marmoreal/); [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2014/04/16/marmoreal-max-lamb-camouflaged-furniture-installation-dzek-milan-2014/amp/)

### Inferences
- Floor: precast terrazzo tiles or slabs (Agglotech Verde Scuro or a custom green ground with Verde Alpi and Giallo chips) are easier to procure, ship and replace in Baku than poured-in-situ terrazzo, which needs a specialist crew. Brass divider strips can be added to precast tiles to draw the neo-deco geometry and lotus fan pattern.
- Cement terrazzo, like marble, contains calcite chips, so it will etch. It needs a penetrating sealer plus a periodic maintenance coat in F&B.
- Marmoreal Black is a strong option for a cake-display plinth or tabletops (its Giallo Mori chips echo the gold). Price tier: high.
- Price tier: Agglotech is mid; Marmoreal is high.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on Mipa or Diespeker green or gold chip products. Diespeker is a UK terrazzo specialist and Mipa is an Italian terrazzo/agglomerate maker **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**.
- No sourced information on "gold" chips (e.g., brass or mother-of-pearl aggregate).

## 4. Brass and metals: unlacquered, lacquered and PVD brass, champagne stainless

### Takeaway
For high-touch F&B surfaces (counter edges, kick plates, vitrine frames, door pulls), **PVD-coated brushed gold or champagne stainless steel** with an anti-fingerprint layer is the lowest-maintenance option. It does not patina. Unlacquered brass should be limited to items staff can polish (e.g., a few handles) because it tarnishes. No sourced F&B maintenance data was found for unlacquered brass.

### Cited Findings
- PVD increases surface hardness and scratch resistance — [John Desmond](https://johndesmond.com/products-and-services/coloured-steel-pvd-coating)
- Anti-fingerprint nano-coatings on coloured stainless steel claim lower cleaning cost — [Hidayath (anti-finger)](https://hidayath.com/architectural-products/metal-finishes/anti-finger/) (UAE-based supplier; manufacturer claim)
- PVD care:
  - Avoid chloride, acidic or alkaline detergents and avoid abrasives.
  - Apply cleaner with a cloth rather than spraying.
  - PVD does not patina.
  - Source: [D Line PVD maintenance guide](https://dline.com/maintenance-guides/maintenace-guide-pvd)
- A brushed gold texture diffuses light and hides fingerprints — [Vigo](https://vigoindustries.com/pages/faucet-finishes-that-resist-fingerprints)
- Foster (Italy) makes PVD gold sinks and fittings, claimed to resist fingerprints and abrasion — [Foster](https://fosterspa.com/en-ww/gold.aspx)
- Precedent: Monvínic restaurant in Barcelona uses coloured stainless steel ("The Inox in Color") — [Archilovers](https://archilovers.com/projects/46349/restaurante-monvinic.html)

### Inferences
- Specify "champagne" or "light gold" PVD on 304 stainless (316 is better near any wet-wash zones), hairline or brushed finish, with an anti-fingerprint coating. Brushed hides scratches better than mirror.
- Use mirror PVD only on small jewel-like vitrine trims.
- The gold tone must be matched across suppliers. Ask for physical samples, because PVD "champagne" varies between rose-champagne and pale gold.
- Turkish (Istanbul) and UAE (Sharjah/Dubai) metal fabricators routinely supply PVD stainless sheet. This is a practical, low-lead-time source for Baku. Price tier: mid.
- Unlacquered brass will darken quickly from Caspian humidity and salty air, and from hand contact. Lacquered brass avoids tarnish but the lacquer wears and chips at touch points. PVD is preferred at touch points.

### Gaps
- No sourced F&B cleaning-cycle or warranty data for unlacquered or lacquered brass.
- No named Turkish or UAE PVD sheet supplier was verified, apart from Hidayath (an anti-finger page).

## 5. Glass: reeded/fluted, tinted green, etched lotus, glass block

### Takeaway
Pilkington's **Reeded** (Privacy 2, designed 1933) and **Cotswold** (Privacy 5, 1964) are documented patterned glasses, available toughened or laminated (deep patterns may not be toughenable). **Seves Glassblock**, which absorbed Vitrablok, makes a 19×19×8 cm block in green tints. It fits a neo-deco glowing wall.

### Cited Findings
- Pilkington Texture Glass:
  - Cotswold is Privacy 5 (most obscure), designed by Brian Yale in 1964. Reeded is Privacy 2, designed by M.L. Anderson in 1933.
  - Toughened or laminated versions are available.
  - Sources: [Pilkington UK pattern visualiser](https://www.pilkington.com/en-gb/uk/householders/decorative-glazing/visualiser); [Pilkington Texture Glass](https://www.pilkington.com/en/xx/products/product-categories/decoration/pilkington-texture-glass); [Pilkington PDF](https://www.pilkington.com/en-gb/uk/householders/~/media/8579EA2FD8494276B2FFFE4625D0A292.ashx)
- Some deep patterns cannot be toughened. Patterned glass is typically 4, 5 or 6 mm thick — [Thermosash](https://thermosash.co.nz/materials/glass/patterned-glass/)
- Seves Glassblock:
  - Combines the Vetroarredo, Vitrablok and Solaris brands. Standard module is 19×19×8 cm.
  - Basic pale range: 7 colours including **Green**, in Wave design, transparent or sandblasted one or both sides.
  - Mendini Collection (16 colours) and New Colour Collection (16 shades) also exist.
  - Sources: [Seves company](https://www.sevesglassblock.com/company/); [Seves Grey 1919/8 Wave (example product page)](https://www.sevesglassblock.com/product/grey-19198-wave/); [Archello](https://archello.com/brand/seves-glassblock)

### Inferences
- For the vitrine back-panel or partition screens, use toughened or laminated Reeded glass (not Cotswold). Reeded gives the vertical fluting that matches the neo-deco aesthetic and keeps the cakes visible as soft silhouettes.
- An etched or sandblasted lotus motif can be added by any glass processor on laminated low-iron glass. The cake vitrine itself must be **low-iron** (e.g., Pilkington Optiwhite or Guardian UltraClear **[product names UNVERIFIED this session]**), so cake colours are not distorted green.
- A green tint belongs only on decorative, non-food-display glass.
- Glass block: Seves Green Wave on a feature wall or a backlit arch infill. Price tier: mid. Italian origin; widely distributed in Turkey **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Climate: if the vitrine faces a street window, use solar-control or low-e glazing on the facade to limit heat gain on refrigerated displays in Baku summers.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm a specific "Fluted 8mm" product by name, or a 6 mm toughened Cotswold datasheet.
- No sourced green-tinted architectural glass product (e.g., AGC or Guardian tints).

## 6. Fabrics: performance velvet in emerald, mohair

### Takeaway
**Kvadrat Harald** (100% cotton velour, 100,000 Martindale) is very durable but has no emerald listed. It is noted as unavailable in the UK/USA, though possibly available elsewhere. **Crypton**-treated velvets (Kravet lines, Crypton Home Lush Emerald) offer stain and moisture resistance. **Harlequin Performance Velvet** (100% polyester, 70,000 Martindale) is a documented performance option. No verified emerald SKU with a Martindale figure from Dedar, Pierre Frey, Romo or Designers Guild was found.

### Cited Findings
- Kvadrat Harald:
  - 100% cotton velour, 100,000 Martindale.
  - Colourways by Raf Simons include dark mint green.
  - Listed as not available in the UK and USA.
  - Sources: [Upholstery Collection 2022 PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0638/4395/files/UPHOLSTERY_COLLECTION_2022_-_220427.pdf); [Galerie Mobler](https://www.galerie-mobler.com/en/design-scandinave/upholstery-fabric-per-meter-kvadrat-foss-30-colours_6635.html)
- Harlequin (Sanderson Design Group) Performance Velvet: 100% polyester, 70,000 Martindale — [Harlequin](https://harlequin.sandersondesigngroup.com/product/fabric/performance-velvet/performance-velvet/hiov134148/). Moss colour listing — [Chairish](https://www.chairish.com/product/23311383/harlequin-performance-velvets-fabric-in-moss)
- Kravet Crypton velvet 34624-8: 100% polyester, 100,000 Wyzenbeek double rubs. Colour is not confirmed as emerald — [Kravet](https://www.kravet.com/34624-8)
- Crypton Lush emerald velvet-chenille — [FabricGuru](https://www.fabricguru.com/crypton/crypton-lush-high-performance-velvet-chenille-upholstery-fabric-emerald-green-6.html); Crypton Home Lush Emerald, 50,000 double rubs (out of stock) — [1502 Fabrics](https://1502fabrics.com/?p=30999)
- Kovi Fabrics emerald Crypton velvet: poly-acrylic, above 200,000 double rubs — [Kovi](https://www.kovifabrics.com/search/product-detail/73622)
- Dedar Velvets collection includes wool, silk, cotton, mohair and linen velvets. No Martindale figure was found — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1736748)

### Inferences
- Banquette and seating: use polyester performance velvet in emerald (Crypton or similar) of at least 50,000 Martindale, or at least 100,000 double rubs Wyzenbeek, for commercial seating. Treat any natural cotton velvet with a stain repellent and fit removable covers.
- Mohair velvet (e.g., Dedar) is luxurious and naturally resilient, but costly. Reserve it for back cushions or low-contact panels. Price tier: high.
- Pick a deep bottle-emerald rather than a bright emerald so it holds near #0F2A22 in warm 2700K light.
- Climate: polyester velvet resists humidity-related crush and mildew better than cotton or silk in Caspian humidity.
- Check commercial fire rating (EN 1021-1/2 or BS 5852) with the supplier.

### Gaps
- No verified emerald SKUs with Martindale data from Dedar, Pierre Frey, Designers Guild, Romo or Kravet.
- No mohair-specific product data.

## 7. Wall finishes: Venetian plaster/marmorino/tadelakt, limewash, fluted panels, paint colours, ceramic tiles

### Takeaway
Viero (Marmorin, Marmorin Hydro, Marmorin Sand), Oikos (Marmorino Naturale, with Superfinish 24 topcoat for high-traffic areas) and Novacolor (Marmorino KS, Archi+ Pietra in NHL 3.5) are documented lime-plaster systems. Farrow & Ball **Studio Green No. 93** (LRV about 7, available in Full Gloss for a lacquer look) and **Green Smoke No. 47** are verified. Little Greene **Invisible Green (56)** and **Puck (298)** are verified. **Bisazza** green glass mosaics and **Mutina** green porcelain (Tape, Rombini, Mews, Primavera) are documented.

### Cited Findings
**Lime plasters**

### Inferences
- **Walls:** Viero or Oikos marmorino in a deep emerald tint close to #0F2A22, burnished and waxed or topcoated (Oikos Superfinish 24) to resist hand marks. Al Gurg (UAE) distributes Viero, which is a practical route to Baku. Price tier: mid–high, mostly labour.
- **Lacquer look:** Farrow & Ball Studio Green in Full Gloss on joinery, or a spray-applied 2K polyurethane lacquer colour-matched to #0F2A22 for counters and fluted panels (more durable in F&B). Note that Studio Green reads near-black and greyish (#474D4A), so it is lighter and greyer than #0F2A22. Little Greene Invisible Green is a closer "deep green" candidate. Have colour matching done by a lab.
- Fluted MDF or solid-wood wall panels in 2K green lacquer with PVD champagne trims work well for the neo-deco arches.
- Limewash (e.g., Bauwerk) is too soft and powdery for touch zones. Use it only on high walls or ceilings.
- Tiles: Mutina Tape Green or Bisazza green glass mosaic suit a splashback or a jewel-box niche behind the vitrine.
- Climate: lime plasters are vapour-open, which suits Caspian humidity. Avoid tadelakt or marmorino directly behind sinks unless sealed.
