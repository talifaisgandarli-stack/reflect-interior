# Lighting, FF&E, Cake-Display Equipment and Lotus Motif: SPRING Cake Boutique, Baku

Context: deep emerald #0F2A22 + champagne gold, line-art lotus logo, historic limestone facade, narrow deep unit. Concept: green-gold heritage luxury, neo-deco arches, jewel-box cake vitrine, lotus motif.
Research date: 2026-10-19. Method: Firecrawl search/scrape + web search, about 20 calls. The Firecrawl rate limit cut some queries short. Product claims rest on manufacturer or retailer pages. Retailer specs often conflict, so confirm everything against manufacturer data sheets before specifying.

## 1. Lighting: products and patisserie lighting specs

### Takeaway
The lighting spec for display is well settled: 3000K for cakes and pastry (2700K for seating), CRI >90 with R9 >50, a 3:1 to 5:1 accent-to-ambient ratio, UGR <19, and concealed in-case LEDs balanced against the room so the glass doesn't mirror. The only verified lotus luminaires are a bespoke Lasvit "Lotus Flower" chandelier, a Castro Lighting "Lotus" collection, a Danish "Design By Us" Lotus pendant, and 1stDibs maker pieces. No Lalique lotus light was found. GUBI Multi-Lite is a verified, easy-to-buy brass decorative pendant.

### Cited Findings: lighting design specs
- Bakery/pastry display LED: "a warm light from 2700K – 3000K and >90Ra CRI will enhance the colour of the bread and pastry". LED also has "minimal dehydration effect due to no IR". — [Nualight, Bakery & Pastry](https://www.nualight.com/applications/refrigeration/bakery/)
- Pastry and cake displays: "3000K lighting with extremely high CRI and R9 values. Soft, diffused lighting combined with subtle directional accents". 2700K "imparts a slight yellow cast to pure white frostings, fresh cream… white marble". — [Archlior, Bakery Lighting Guide (2026)](https://archlior.com/blog/bakery-lighting-design-guide)
- Spec "CRI > 90 with an R9 > 50" for display areas. TM-30 with Rg slightly above 100 gently oversaturates warm colours. — [Archlior](https://archlior.com/blog/bakery-lighting-design-guide)
- Ambient 300–500 lux. Accent at least 3:1 over ambient, 5:1 for a "dramatic, high-end retail feel". Cash desk/counter 500 lux. Kitchen 4000K at 500–750 lux. Seating: lower levels, low-glare pendants or 2700K sconces. — [Archlior](https://archlior.com/blog/bakery-lighting-design-guide)
- Glare: UGR <19. Use deep-recessed optics, snoots/barn doors and honeycomb louvres. Beam angles: 10–15° for a hero cake, 24–36° as the "workhorse" for pastry displays. — [Archlior](https://archlior.com/blog/bakery-lighting-design-guide)
- Glass vitrines: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection, so ceiling spots aimed at sloped case glass bounce glare at customers. Put track heads behind the customer's sightline or use integrated lighting. Hide in-case linear LEDs behind opaque extrusions so no LED dots reflect in glossy glazes. If the case interior is darker than the room, "the glass will act like a mirror". — [Archlior](https://archlior.com/blog/bakery-lighting-design-guide)
- Controls: DALI or Casambi scenes (e.g. a "morning" vs. a dimmed "afternoon" café scene). Specify L90 >50,000 h lumen maintenance. — [Archlior](https://archlior.com/blog/bakery-lighting-design-guide)
- Specialist food-display lamp makers for in-case lighting: Nualight (refrigeration LED rails) — [Nualight](https://www.nualight.com/applications/refrigeration/bakery/); Promolux (bakery application lamps) — [Promolux](https://www.promolux.com/applications/food-retail-displays/bakery/)

### Inferences
- Lighting layers for a narrow, deep unit: (1) integrated 3000K, CRI 95/R9 >50 linear LED in the vitrine behind brass/opaque extrusions; (2) 24–36° recessed or track accents with honeycomb louvres, aimed from behind the customer's line to the case glass; (3) decorative pendants and sconces at 2700K in the seating zone (a lotus statement piece, e.g. bespoke Lasvit or the Castro Lotus suspension, over a hero table or under an arch); (4) a DALI/Casambi scene set. At a 3:1–5:1 ratio, a 300–400 lux ambient puts the vitrine at about 1,000–1,500 lux. That figure is my calculation, not from a source.
- The 2700K cast on white cream and marble supports 3000K inside the vitrine even if the seating runs at 2700K.
- GUBI Multi-Lite in brass or midnight blue-brass fits the green-gold deco palette and is stock-available through EU retailers. A bespoke Lasvit piece needs a project-sales enquiry and a long lead time.

- No IES RP-2 (retail) or EN 12464-1 lux tables were retrieved directly. The lux values above come from one practitioner guide (Archlior), not the standard itself.
- No Baku/Azerbaijan dealer was found for GUBI or Flos. Mozaik in Istanbul was cited as a Flos distributor — [Time Out Istanbul](https://www.timeout.com/istanbul/tr/alisveris-stil/mozaik-4). ## 2. Cake display vitrines / refrigerated showcases

### Takeaway
The Italian and Portuguese makers (ISA, Orion, IFI, Jordão) all publish pastry ranges. Jordão's MINIMAL is the one verified source of the cake spec: +2…+4 °C and up to 80% RH with a humidification option. Ventilated cases dry cakes faster, so for cream cakes specify humidity control or static/low-velocity air. No off-the-shelf "brass jewel-box" refrigerated case was found. Plan a custom brass-clad joinery shell around an OEM refrigerated module, or a bespoke enquiry to the makers.

### Cited Findings
- **ISA (Italy)** pastry range includes Pentagram Display / Alpha / Beta, Stratos Pas ("high-end… maximum technical performance with aesthetics"), Kaleido Pas (ventilated, "Full Mode" system), Millennium Pas (ventilated, finned evaporator), Diva Pas, 3D Show Pas and Cristal Tower. Pentagram Alpha is described as "a real treasure chest" for refined display. — [ISA pastry display cases](https://www.isaitaly.com/en/ice-cream-pastry/pastry-display-cases/). UK distribution via Taylor UK — [Taylor UK](https://www.taylor-company.co.uk/isa-refrigerated-display-cabinets/)
- **Orion (Italy)**: display cases for pastry and gelato. — [Orion Style showcases](https://www.orionstyle.com/en/orion-style-showcases/). OneShow (720 mm deep) is ISA/Orion-related per Pinterest; ISA also lists "Oneshow Free" for ice cream and pastry — [ISA](https://www.isaitaly.com/en/prodotto/ice-cream-pastry/ice-cream-display-cases/oneshow-free/)
- **IFI (Italy)**: pastry and pralines sector cases. — [IFI pastry](https://www.ifi.it/en/sectors/pastry-and-pralines/)
- **Jordão (Portugal) MINIMAL** counter case: pastry version at +2…+4 °C (3M2 class) with an optional humidification system up to 80% RH. — [ArchiExpo / Jordão MINIMAL](https://archiexpo.com/prod/jordao/product-142614-2227050.html) (page fetch failed; data from the search excerpt)
- Ventilated cases: cakes and sandwiches "may dry out more quickly". — [Roller Grill](https://www.rollergrill-international.com/en/blog/advices/210-how-do-commercial-refrigerated-pastry-display-cases-maintain-the-freshness-and-quality-of-baked-goods.html)
- Cream/custard cakes: store at 2–5 °C — [Industry Kitchens](https://www.industrykitchens.com.au/commercial-refrigeration/fridges/display-fridges-1/cake-display-cabinet/refrigerated-cake-display). Typical pastry fridge range +2 to +8 °C — [GN.ge guide (Georgia)](https://gn.ge/en/how-to-choose-a-pastry-refrigerator/)
- Humidity ratings vary: Bartscher cake case max 75% RH — [Tingstad](https://www.tingstad.com/se-en/servingware/cooling/display-fridges/bartscher-tartmonter-700314); Bonvue rated for 90% ambient RH — [Harvey Norman](https://www.harveynorman.com.au/bonvue-commercial-chilled-cake-display.html)
- Example wooden-clad Italian pastry case: ventilated, +2/+10 °C — [Ristoattrezzature PDF](https://www.ristoattrezzature.com/en/bspk-catalog/product_pdf/index/id/9570/); Ristoservice ventilated pastry case with finned evaporator and HCFC/HFC-free PU insulation — [Ristoservice](https://www.ristoservicesrl.com/en/bakery-display-cabinets/654/)

### Inferences
- Brief for the jewel-box vitrine: +2…+4 °C, 75–85% RH with humidification, heated/anti-fog double glazing, low-velocity or static air for cream cakes, 3000K CRI 95 LED behind brass profiles, and a remote condensing unit if the narrow unit can take one (less heat and noise). Clad the plinth and frame in brushed or champagne brass PVD stainless, which holds up better than solid brass in a cold, humid case.
- A cube/pedestal "hero" case for a single signature cake fits the jewel-box idea. ISA Cristal Tower or a custom bell-jar case are possible bases.

### Gaps
- Brandford, Pastorfrigor, Koxka, Hiber, Arneg, Bocchini and Costan product pages were not reached (rate limit). Searches for bespoke brass-finish showcases from Orion, Brandford or IFI returned nothing. Ask the makers directly about custom RAL/brass cladding.
- No confirmed anti-fog/heated-glass spec sheet was retrieved for any named model.
- Azerbaijan distributors for ISA, Orion, IFI or Jordão were not verified. A Georgian (Tbilisi) equipment retailer (gn.ge) writes about pastry fridges, which suggests a Caucasus supply channel. Unverified.

## 4. Lotus motif: history and contemporary applications

### Takeaway
The lotus was a key motif of Egyptian revival. Napoleon's 1798 expedition started the fascination, and Carter's 1922 opening of Tutankhamun's tomb set off the 1920s wave, in which Art Deco reworked the lotus as geometric, frontal and architectonic. That supports a line-art, symmetrical, deco-geometric lotus over a naturalistic one. The closest pastry precedent is Cédric Grolet Opéra's ceiling: a "lake" of handmade paper water lilies by Mathilde Nivet, lit by rhythmic pendants. A restaurant precedent (Imperial Spice, India) has an inverted lotus pond of metal lotus modules in the ceiling.

### Cited Findings
- Tutankhamun's tomb was opened in late 1922 by Howard Carter. By early 1923, Egyptian-inspired garments, accessories and decoration had followed. Egyptian floral designs and the lotus in particular were admired. — [ASU FIDM Museum, "Egyptomania"](https://asufidmmuseum.asu.edu/learn/articles/egyptomania)
- The Art Deco lotus is "geometric, frontal, and architectonic", unlike naturalistic Art Nouveau treatments. Interest dates back to Napoleon's 1798 expedition. — [Skyjems encyclopedia: lotus motif](https://skyjems.ca/pages/encyclopedia-lotus-motif) (dealer glossary, secondary); Egyptian revival glossary — [The Cartiers](https://www.the-cartiers.com/glossary/egyptian-revival/); [Adin](https://www.antiquejewel.com/glossary/egyptian-revival-jewellery-tutankhamun-phase)
- Lotus motifs became popular again in Art Deco architecture and decorative arts. Example: a pair of Italian Art Deco carved-marble lotus-form planters, c.1930. — [Decorative Collective](https://www.decorativecollective.com/product/chris-holmes-antiques/pair-of-italian-art-deco-carved-marble-lotus-form-planters-c1930-lotuspla/print)
- **Cédric Grolet Opéra (Paris)**: "On the ceiling, a series of delicate hanging lamps illuminate in rhythm, a lake of paper handmade water lilies, designed by the artist Mathilde Nivet", in grey to light pink and shaped after Grolet's signature flower pastry. The Haussmannian shell was kept (stone wall, mouldings, stucco rosette). Forged-iron handrail with a vegetal drawing. Big golden round mirror above the bar. — [Archilovers](https://www.archilovers.com/projects/264594/cedric-grolet-opera.html); Neolith surfaces used — [Neolith](https://www.neolith.com/neolith-projects/neolith-cedric-grolet-paris)
- **Imperial Spice (India, Amit Sharma & Rupal Rajan)**: an inverted lotus pond in the ceiling. Dim yellow light on metal lotus-shaped modules recreates sunlight on water. — [The Design Awards](https://thedesignawards.co.uk/hotel-property-entrants-2019-closed/imperial-spice-axiom-india)
- **Hanoi Lotus Centre (Decibel Architecture, proposed)**: the circulation ceiling takes its colours and patterns from the underside of a lotus leaf, with coloured skylights and light boxes. — [BD+C](https://www.bdcnetwork.com/building-sector-reports/cultural-facilities/news/55160775/the-hanoi-lotus-centre-will-bloom-from-the-middle-of-a-lake)
- Waterjet stone medallions made to CAD specs with floral scrollwork are an established technique. — [Caron Stone](https://www.caronstone.com/de/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/34655). Marcel Wanders' home has a laser-cut ornamental parquet by Berti — [ArchiExpo Trends / Berti](https://trends.archiexpo.com/berti-pavimenti-legno/project-55657-296926.html). **No Wanders lotus project was found.**

### Inferences
- Ways to apply the logo's line-art lotus in deco-geometric form: (a) a brass-strip lotus inlay medallion at the threshold, set into terrazzo or waterjet marble (Verde Guatemala/Nero Marquina with Calacatta); (b) sandblasted or acid-etched lotus line-art on the vitrine end glass and the arch glazing; (c) a perforated or pressed brass lotus-lattice screen to divide the counter from the seating; (d) a ceiling feature over the seating, either a Grolet-style "lake" of sculpted petals or the Imperial Spice inverted pond of metal modules, lit at 2700K; (e) lotus statement lighting (Lasvit bespoke or Castro Lotus); (f) gold-foil line-art lotus on emerald packaging and on cake boards. Items (a) to (f) are design proposals, not sourced products.
- A paper installation like Grolet's suits only a sealed ceiling zone away from humidity and food-hygiene areas. Metal or glass is more practical for a cake shop. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- No verified link for a "Wanders lotus" project. No verified Lalique lotus luminaire, though Lalique has historic lotus glassware that was not checked.
- No built example of a brass lotus floor inlay was found with a citable link.
- No architectural Egyptian-revival lotus building examples with sources (e.g. 1920s cinemas or Carreras Building London) were retrieved. They need follow-up.
- Lotus tableware or packaging brand references were not researched due to the call budget.
